I'm a Product Owner at ABB Drives in Helsinki, with 25+ years in technology behind me. The professional version of me lives on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anupamarohi/).

I wrote Live Version in 2014. I kept commenting out code I wasn't ready to delete and zipping folders I was afraid to lose, and neither habit was serving me. LV was the answer: versioning that runs itself, for the messy experimental work that never deserves a team commit. It has been quietly backing up my work ever since.

Since 2019 I have facilitated a weekly public guided meditation group in Helsinki. Anyone can join.
